It's not only about fees

Poshmark brings strong built-in social/party discovery and a buyer base that expects bundled shipping, but its 20% cut is steep and haggling is common.

Depop is far cheaper and skews younger and trend-led, which is ideal for vintage and streetwear, but you do more of the marketing and photography yourself and may pay to Boost listings for visibility.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Poshmark or Depop?

Depop is almost always cheaper — no commission, just a small payment-processing fee. On a $50 US sale, Poshmark takes $10 (20%) while Depop takes roughly $2.10.

When can Poshmark be the cheaper option?

On very low-priced items under $15. Poshmark's flat $2.95 can edge out Depop's percentage-plus-fixed fee on the cheapest listings, so run the numbers rather than assuming.

Did Depop bring back its 10% fee?

No — the commission was removed in July 2024 and has not returned. The only standard cost is payment processing; Boost is entirely optional.
