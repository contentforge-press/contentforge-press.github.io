What's included in Etsy's fees?

Listing fee ($0.20): Charged when you create a listing. Each listing lasts 4 months or until it sells. If it doesn't sell and you renew, you pay $0.20 again.

Transaction fee (6.5%): Charged on the total sale amount including item price, shipping, and gift wrap. This is Etsy's main commission.

Payment processing (3% + $0.25): Etsy Payments handles the transaction. This covers credit card processing and deposit to your bank.

Regulatory operating fee (0.30% + $0.02): A newer fee (introduced 2022) that covers Etsy's regulatory compliance costs. Charged on the total sale amount.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $25 sale?

On a $25 sale in 2026, Etsy charges $2.93 total: $0.20 listing + $1.63 transaction + $1.00 payment processing + $0.10 regulatory operating. You keep $22.07 before COGS and shipping.

What percentage does Etsy take from each sale?

Etsy's total fee is approximately 10-11% of the sale price for most US sales. This includes all mandatory fees. The effective rate decreases at higher price points because the fixed $0.20 listing and $0.25 payment processing become a smaller percentage.

Are Etsy fees worth it?

Etsy provides a built-in audience of 90+ million active buyers specifically looking for handmade, vintage, and craft items. If your products fit those categories and you can maintain healthy margins at 10-11% fees, Etsy's traffic makes it worthwhile. Compare with eBay or Amazon Handmade.

Do I pay Etsy fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 0.30% regulatory fee are both charged on the total amount the buyer pays, which includes shipping. Only the payment processing fee (3% + $0.25) is charged on item price only.
