Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Shopify plan in 2026?

The Basic plan starts at $39/month and includes Shopify Payments online card processing at 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.

Are there fees beyond the monthly plan?

Yes. You pay card processing on each order, and paid apps or themes add cost. Using a third-party payment gateway instead of Shopify Payments adds a 2% / 1% / 0.6% surcharge depending on your plan.

Do I need Shopify Payments?

It is the default and usually the lowest-friction option. If you use another gateway you avoid the card rate but pay an additional percentage on each order.
