When does a Store subscription pay for itself?

If you list more than 250 items/month, compare the cost of no-store insertion fees vs. a store subscription. At 500 listings/month with no store, you'd pay 250 × $0.35 = $87.50/month in insertion fees — a Basic store ($21.95 + 250 × $0.25 = $84.45) saves you a bit. At 1,000+ listings, the savings grow fast.

Frequently asked questions

How many free listings do I get on eBay?

Every eBay seller gets 250 zero insertion fee listings per month. Store subscribers get more: Starter 250, Basic 1,000+, Premium 10,000+, Anchor 25,000+, Enterprise 100,000+.

What is the eBay insertion fee after free listings?

After using your monthly free listing allocation, the insertion fee is $0.35 per listing for non-store sellers and Starter stores. Store subscribers pay less: Basic $0.25, Premium $0.10, Anchor $0.05, Enterprise $0.05.

Is it free to list on eBay?

Yes, for most casual sellers. eBay gives 250 free listings per month. You only pay a $0.35 insertion fee per listing after exceeding that allowance. The final value fee (13.6% for most categories) is still charged when your item sells.

Do Good 'Til Cancelled listings count against my free allowance?

Yes. Each monthly renewal of a Good 'Til Cancelled listing counts as one listing against your free allocation. If you have 250 GTC listings and no store, you've used your entire free allowance every month.
