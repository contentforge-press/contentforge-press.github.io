Three levels of verified real

Every level requires control of something a stranger cannot fake, signed offline with a key only RealProof holds.

1

Verified Contact

Control of a real email or account. Establishes a reachable, accountable point of contact.

2

Verified Presence

Control of a domain or a linked, operating store on a real marketplace (Amazon, Etsy, eBay and others).

3

Verified Business

Domain plus operating stores/history and an accountable human or entity behind the name.

Why a neutral layer

Platforms mark their own homework

A badge from one marketplace never tells you the full, cross-platform story and isn’t neutral.

Blockchain never reached normal businesses

Protocols require tokens and wallets that ordinary sellers and platforms will not adopt.

Hardware liveness is exclusionary

Biometric checks raise privacy barriers and exclude over a billion people without formal ID.

AI makes the problem worse — and more valuable

As synthetic listings, reviews and agents multiply, verifiable authenticity becomes the scarcest asset.
