Frequently asked questions

Does Walmart charge a monthly fee?

No. Unlike Amazon's Professional plan, Walmart Marketplace has zero setup, subscription or monthly fees. You only pay a referral fee on each completed sale.

What is the referral fee calculated on?

The total sales price, which includes the item price plus shipping and handling, gift wrap and other charges. This calculator uses the item price for a simple estimate.

Is WFS cheaper than Amazon FBA?

Walmart reports WFS averages about 15% lower per-item fulfillment cost than the main competitor, and it includes a 2-day delivery tag that can lift conversion. Compare your specific size and weight to be sure.

How hard is it to get approved?

Walmart vets sellers more strictly — you generally need a US business tax ID, verifiable address, e-commerce or marketplace history, GS1 UPCs and US-based fulfillment with returns. It is aimed at professional sellers rather than casual hobby accounts.
