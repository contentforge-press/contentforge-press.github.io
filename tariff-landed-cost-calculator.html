  • Why is my duty higher than other websites show? After July 24, 2026 the expired Section 122 was replaced by a forced-labor tariff (12.5% for China), which stacks on top of base MFN, China Section 301 and any Section 232. Tools showing only the base rate understate the real cost.
  • Do I still need an HTS code? For a quick estimate, no. For the legally binding rate, yes — the exact 10-digit code, plus any AD/CVD orders, is what CBP actually applies.
  • What is not included? Anti-dumping/countervailing duties, temporary Chapter 99 measures, excise and FDA user fees, insurance, and drayage/warehousing — add those separately for full door cost.
