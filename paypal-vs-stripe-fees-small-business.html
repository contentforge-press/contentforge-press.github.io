Frequently asked questions

Is Stripe cheaper than PayPal?

On standard US online transactions Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 is lower than PayPal's 3.49% + $0.49, especially on small-ticket sales where the fixed fee matters.

Do both charge monthly fees?

Standard pay-as-you-go accounts have no monthly fee for either provider; you only pay per transaction.

Are there extra fees for international cards?

Yes. Both providers add an additional percentage for cross-border payments and currency conversion, which can narrow or reverse the difference.
