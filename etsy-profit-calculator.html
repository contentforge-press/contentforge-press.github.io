How to read your Etsy profit

The total fee load on a typical Etsy sale is roughly 9.5–10.5% when Offsite Ads don't apply (listing + transaction + payment + regulatory). For a $35 item with $5 shipping: listing $0.20, transaction $2.60 (6.5% of $40), payment $3.45 (3% of $40 + $0.25), regulatory $0.14 (0.3% of $40 + $0.02) = $6.39 total, or 16% of the $40 gross.

If a sale comes through Offsite Ads, add another 12–15% of the sale price. For sellers under $10K/year in revenue, Offsite Ads are optional — you can opt out in your shop settings. Once you cross $10K, enrollment becomes mandatory at the lower 12% rate.

What to price at for your target profit

The break-even price shown above is the minimum sale price to earn $1 profit after all fees and costs. To hit a specific profit target, work backwards: Price = (Cost + Target Profit + Fixed Fees) ÷ (1 − Fee Percentages). For most items with 6.5% + 3% + 0.3% variable and $0.20 + $0.25 + $0.02 fixed, the all-in rate is about 10% variable + ~$0.47 fixed.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from each sale in 2026?

Roughly 9.5–10.5% plus $0.47 in fixed fees per transaction (listing $0.20 + payment $0.25 + regulatory $0.02). On a $35 item with $5 shipping, total fees are about $6.39 before Offsite Ads.

What changed with Etsy fees in June 2026?

Etsy updated its regulatory operating fee structure effective June 22, 2026. The base rates (listing, transaction, payment processing) remained the same. This calculator reflects the latest published rates.

Is the Offsite Ads fee mandatory?

If your shop earned under $10,000 in the last 365 days, Offsite Ads are optional — you can opt out anytime. Once you cross $10K, enrollment becomes mandatory at 12%. Before $10K, the rate is 15%.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Both the 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% payment processing fee apply to the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping and any gift wrapping charges.

How do I increase my Etsy profit margin?

Raise prices (Etsy buyers expect to pay — they comparison-shop within the marketplace), reduce packaging costs, ship lighter, and opt out of Offsite Ads if eligible. Use our AI Pricing Copilot to find the optimal price across all platforms simultaneously.
