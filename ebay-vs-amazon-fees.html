When is eBay cheaper than Amazon?

For most categories at typical prices ($25–$200), eBay's flat 13.6% is lower than Amazon's 15% referral fee. At $50, eBay charges about $7.10 vs Amazon's $7.80. The gap narrows in lower-rate Amazon categories like electronics (8%) where Amazon charges only $4.30 on a $50 sale.

Amazon's $39.99/mo Professional plan matters: if you sell fewer than 40 items/month, the Individual plan at $0.99/item may be cheaper overall. Above 40 sales/month, the Pro plan's per-item cost amortizes to under $1.

FBA fulfillment adds $3–$7+ per unit on Amazon. If you self-ship on eBay, your actual total cost may be similar. Factor in Prime eligibility on Amazon — it can boost sales volume enough to offset higher fees.

Frequently asked questions

Is eBay cheaper than Amazon for sellers in 2026?

For most categories, yes — eBay's 13.6% final value fee is lower than Amazon's 15% referral. On a $50 sale, eBay costs $7.10 vs Amazon's $7.80. But Amazon's lower-rate categories (electronics at 8%, baby ≤$10 at 8%) can be cheaper than eBay.

Does eBay charge fees on shipping?

Yes. eBay's final value fee applies to the total sale amount including shipping charges the buyer pays. This means a $50 item with $8 shipping incurs the 13.6% fee on $58, not $50.

Does Amazon charge fees on shipping too?

Yes. Amazon's referral fee applies to the total sales price including item price, delivery charges, and gift wrapping. This is the same principle as eBay's fee base.

Which is better: eBay Individual or Amazon Professional plan?

If you sell under 40 items/month, eBay's free listing tier (250/mo free) is cheapest. On Amazon, the Individual plan at $0.99/item is better under 40 sales. Above that, Amazon Professional ($39.99/mo) amortizes to under $1/sale.

What about FBA costs on Amazon?

FBA adds $3.22–$6.10+ per unit depending on size/weight, plus monthly storage ($0.56–$2.40/cubic foot). This is on top of the referral fee. If you self-ship on eBay, compare your actual shipping cost to FBA's all-in rate.

Can I sell on both eBay and Amazon?

Yes. Many sellers list on both platforms. eBay is often better for used, vintage, and collectible items. Amazon suits new products, private label, and high-volume SKU-based selling. Cross-listing maximizes reach.
