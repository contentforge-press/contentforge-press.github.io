Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax rate in North Dakota?

The state rate is 500%. Local governments add an average of 209%, so the typical combined rate is about 709%.

Do I have to collect sales tax as an out-of-state seller?

Remote sellers establish economic nexus in North Dakota at $100,000 in sales or 200 transactions. $100,000 or 200 transactions. Once you cross the threshold you generally must register and begin collecting on sales delivered into North Dakota.

How is tax calculated on a sale?

Multiply the pre-tax price by the combined rate (state + local). For example, a $100 sale at the average 709% rate carries $7.09 of tax, for a total of $107.09.
