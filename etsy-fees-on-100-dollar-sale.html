Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $100 sale?

Including the $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 payment fee, and the regulatory operating fee, total fees are about $10.27 on a $100 sale.

Why is the payment processing fee separate?

Etsy charges a payment processing fee to handle the buyer's card or payment method; in the US it is 3% of the amount plus a fixed $0.25.

Does Etsy charge on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the payment processing fee apply to the shipping amount the buyer pays as well.
