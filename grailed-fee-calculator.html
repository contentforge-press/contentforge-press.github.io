Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Grailed take in 2026?

Grailed charges 6% commission with a $1.99 minimum on sale prices below $120, and 9% on sales of $120 or more. Stripe payment processing is charged separately on top.

Is there a fee to list an item?

No. Posting a listing is free; Grailed only charges a commission and payment fee once an item sells.

Does the commission include the payment fee?

No. The commission and the Stripe payment processing fee are separate. Add both together to get the total deducted from your sale price.

Why is my payment fee higher than shown?

The fixed portion can be higher for sellers who have not completed Stripe onboarding, and international buyers carry a higher percentage rate. Use the selector to match your situation.
