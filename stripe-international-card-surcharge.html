Frequently asked questions

Does Stripe charge more for foreign cards?

Yes. Cards issued outside your country incur an additional percentage on top of the standard processing rate.

Is currency conversion a separate fee?

Yes. If a payment is in a currency other than your account currency, Stripe applies a conversion charge.

Should I block international customers?

Usually not. Factor the extra fee into pricing for overseas customers rather than losing those sales.
