Verdict

Vinted is cheaper — you keep 100% of the sale price with zero deductions. Depop's 3.3% + $0.45 processing fee means you lose ~4-7% per sale depending on price.

Depop has the audience for trendy, Gen Z-targeted fashion in the US and UK. If your items sell faster on Depop, the processing fee is worth the volume.

Smart move: List on both at no cost. Vinted for margin, Depop for volume.
