Etsy fees in 2026, explained

Three fees apply to every sale in every country. Two more can apply depending on how the buyer found you and where your bank is.

FeeAmount (2026)Charged on
Listing fee$0.20Each item, when published or renewed (every 4 months), whether or not it sells
Transaction fee6.5%Item price + shipping + gift wrap
Payment processing (US)3% + $0.25Full order total; rate depends on your bank country
Offsite Ads15% or 12%Order total, only when a sale is attributed to an Etsy ad; capped at $100/order
Regulatory operating fee0% – 1.97%Order total; only in certain countries

Offsite Ads rules

Shops with under $10,000 in sales over the trailing 12 months pay 15% and can opt out. Shops at or above $10,000 pay 12%, cannot opt out, and the fee is capped at $100 per order. The 15%/12% is charged on the whole order, including shipping.

What changed on June 22, 2026

Only the regulatory operating fees moved: France 0.47%→1.14%, Italy 0.32%→0.80%, Spain 0.72%→0.88%, UK 0.32%→0.48%; Türkiye 2.27%→1.67%, India 0.29%→0.05%; Hungary added 1.97%. The $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, processing rates and Offsite Ads rates were unchanged.

Sources: Etsy Fees & Payments Policy (etsy.com/legal/fees), verified September 2026; Shopify & independent fee guides.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $100 sale?

For a US seller, about $9.95: $0.20 listing + $6.50 transaction + $3.25 payment processing. You receive about $90.05 before your material, shipping and ad costs. If the order came from an Offsite Ad, add $15 (or $12 for shops over $10k).

What percentage does Etsy take in 2026?

The mandatory stack is $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + about 3–4% payment processing, roughly 10–11% of a typical order. Offsite Ads can add another 12–15% on attributed orders.

Why is payment processing higher for non-US sellers?

Processing is set by the country of your bank account, not the buyer. US accounts pay 3% + $0.25; UK and most European accounts pay 4% plus a fixed amount, and some also pay a regulatory operating fee on every order.

Can I turn off Offsite Ads?

Only if your shop has earned under $10,000 in the last 12 months: Shop Manager → Settings → Offsite Ads. Above $10,000 the program is mandatory for the life of the shop at 12%, capped at $100 per order.

How is the break-even price calculated?

It is the lowest item price at which the Etsy payout (order total minus every fee) exactly covers your material and shipping costs. The target-margin price raises that until your net profit equals the margin you set.

Does this calculator include sales tax or VAT?

US sellers are not charged transaction fees on sales tax, so the default model excludes it. UK/EU sellers may also owe VAT on Etsy's fees themselves; treat the result as pre-tax and confirm with your dashboard.
