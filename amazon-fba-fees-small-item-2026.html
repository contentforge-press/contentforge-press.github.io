Frequently asked questions

What two main fees do FBA sellers pay?

A referral fee (typically 15%) and an FBA fulfillment fee that covers picking, packing, shipping and customer service.

Why does item size matter for FBA?

Fulfillment fees are based on the item's size and weight, so small, light products cost less to fulfill than large or heavy ones.

Is the fulfillment fee the same for every product?

No. It changes with product size tier, weight, and whether the item is standard or oversized.
