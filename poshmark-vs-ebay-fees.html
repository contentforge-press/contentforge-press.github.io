When to choose each platform

Choose eBay when: Selling electronics, collectibles, men's clothing, home goods, or anything over $75 where the 13.6% vs 20% gap really matters. eBay's larger, more diverse buyer pool means faster sales for non-fashion items.

Choose Poshmark when: Selling women's branded fashion (Lululemon, Free People, Anthropologie, designer). The 20% hurts, but Poshmark's fashion-focused audience and social features (sharing, Posh Parties) can drive higher prices and faster sell-through in those categories.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Poshmark or eBay?

On raw fee math, eBay is cheaper at almost every price point above $15. eBay charges ~13.6% + $0.40 per order on the total (item + shipping). Poshmark charges a flat 20% on item price only, but includes a prepaid shipping label. On a $50 item, eBay fees are ~$7.20 vs Poshmark $10.00.

Does Poshmark include shipping in the 20%?

Yes. Poshmark's 20% commission covers payment processing and provides a prepaid shipping label. Your 20% is charged on item price only — not on shipping. On eBay, the 13.6% FVF is charged on the total including what the buyer pays for shipping.

Is eBay better than Poshmark for selling clothes?

For women's branded fashion, Poshmark's audience can drive faster sales at higher prices despite the 20% fee. For men's clothing, electronics, collectibles, and anything non-fashion, eBay's larger buyer pool and lower fees make it the better choice.

What are eBay fees in 2026?

eBay charges a final value fee of 13.6% of the total sale (item + shipping + tax) for most categories, plus a $0.40 per-order fee. First 250 listings per month are free; after that $0.35 per listing.

What are Poshmark fees in 2026?

Poshmark charges no listing fee. On sales under $15: flat $2.95. On sales $15 and over: 20% commission on item price only. Payment processing and a prepaid shipping label are included.
