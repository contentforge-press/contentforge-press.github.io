Fees are only part of the decision

Whatnot gives you live-stream selling tools and an active, impulse-buying audience with no upfront cost — but you must show up and perform on camera, and sales depend on your livestream schedule.

eBay offers a far larger search-driven buyer pool and passive listings that sell while you sleep, but competition and fee types are greater and you generally do your own photography and listing.

Many resellers use both: eBay for steady catalog sales and Whatnot to move volume and build an audience. Price each channel with the calculator to protect your margin.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Whatnot take in 2026?

8% commission in most categories, plus a 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing fee — about 11% combined on a typical sale. Electronics are 5% and coins 4%.

Is Whatnot really cheaper than eBay?

Usually on a straight-fee basis at normal prices. The gap widens a lot on high-value collectibles because Whatnot stops charging commission above $1,500, while eBay's percentage keeps applying.

Do I pay anything if my Whatnot show doesn't sell?

No — no listing fees, no subscription, and nothing on unsold items. You only pay when a sale completes.
