Frequently asked questions

Does an eBay Store lower selling fees?

Yes. Store subscribers generally pay a lower final value fee than non-subscribers, but pay a monthly subscription.

Who should get a Store?

Sellers who list a high enough volume each month that the lower final value fees exceed the subscription cost.

How do I know if it pays off?

Multiply your monthly sales by the fee difference and compare it to the Store subscription price.
