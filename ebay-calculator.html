Frequently asked questions

What is eBay's final value fee in 2026?

Most U.S. categories: 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500 per item and 2.35% above that, plus a $0.30–$0.40 per-order fee.

Does the fee apply to shipping?

Yes — it's calculated on the item price plus shipping, handling and sales tax.

How much are insertion fees?

The first 250 listings per month are free on ebay.com; extras are $0.35 each.

Does a Store lower fees?

Basic and above add free listings and reduce final value fees; exact savings vary by category.
