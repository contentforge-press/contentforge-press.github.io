When to use each

eBay for volume: 700M+ monthly visitors, global reach, auction and fixed-price formats. The fees are high, but so is the traffic.

Bonanza for margin: Use as a supplementary channel. Import from eBay for free, pay 3.5% on any sale. Every Bonanza sale is nearly pure profit compared to eBay.

Smart strategy: List on eBay for traffic. Sync to Bonanza (zero marginal effort). The fee savings on Bonanza are ~10% per sale.
