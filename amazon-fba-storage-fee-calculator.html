Storage utilization surcharge

Amazon may add a surcharge if your inventory stays too long. The surcharge applies when your storage utilization ratio exceeds 22 weeks for standard-size or 30 weeks for oversize. Rates: $0.44–$0.69/cu ft additional.

Tip: Keep inventory turnover under 16 weeks to avoid surcharges. Use the Reorder Point Calculator to time your restocks.
