On a $35 sale, here's the real difference

On a $35 item with $5 shipping (US seller): Etsy charges ~$3.98 total (listing $0.20 + transaction $2.60 + payment $1.30). Amazon Handmade charges $5.25 (15% of $35). Etsy is $1.27 cheaper on this sale — that's 3.6 percentage points.

But Amazon Handmade has two advantages: no listing fees (saves $0.20 per item if you list and relist often), and access to Prime members via FBA. If FBA drives significantly more sales volume, the higher per-unit fee may still produce more total profit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Etsy cheaper than Amazon Handmade?

For most items, yes. Etsy's effective rate is ~10–11% (6.5% transaction + 3% payment processing + small flat fees). Amazon Handmade charges a flat 15% with no separate listing or payment processing fees. On a $35 sale, Etsy saves about $1.27.

Does Amazon Handmade have a monthly fee?

No. Amazon waives the $39.99/month Professional selling plan fee for approved Handmade artisans. You only pay the 15% referral fee when an item sells.

Which platform has more buyers for handmade?

Amazon has 300M+ total shoppers, but the Handmade section is a small part of Amazon's marketplace. Etsy has ~90 million active buyers who specifically search for handmade items. Etsy's conversion rate for handmade is generally higher.

Can I sell on both Etsy and Amazon Handmade?

Yes. Many artisans list on both platforms. There's no exclusivity requirement. Price each item to account for the different fee structures — you'll keep more per sale on Etsy, but Amazon may drive higher volume.

What about Etsy Offsite Ads?

Etsy charges 15% on any sale attributed to their Offsite Ads (12% once you pass $10K/year). This is optional for shops under $10K/yr but mandatory above that threshold. If you get a lot of Offsite Ad sales, Etsy's effective rate can approach or exceed Amazon Handmade's 15%.

Does Amazon Handmade offer FBA?

Yes — approved Handmade sellers can use Fulfillment by Amazon, making products Prime-eligible. FBA adds per-unit fees ($3.22+) and storage costs but can significantly boost visibility and sales to Prime members.
