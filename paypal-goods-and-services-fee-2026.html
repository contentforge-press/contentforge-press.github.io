Frequently asked questions

What is the PayPal goods and services fee?

For US sellers receiving online payments it is 3.49% of the transaction plus a fixed $0.49.

Who pays the fee, buyer or seller?

The seller receiving the payment pays the fee; it is deducted before the money reaches them.

Is there an extra cost for international sales?

Yes, cross-border payments and currency conversion add additional fees on top of the standard rate.
