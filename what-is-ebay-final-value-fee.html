What Is eBay Final Value Fee? (2026 Complete Guide)

eBay's final value fee (FVF) is the commission eBay charges sellers when an item sells. It's calculated as a percentage of the total sale amount, including shipping costs. This is eBay's primary way of making money from sellers.

Quick Answer: eBay final value fees average 13.25% of the total sale amount, plus a $0.30 per-order fee. Most categories range from 12% to 15%. The fee is charged on the item price + shipping + any sales tax.

How to Calculate eBay Final Value Fee

The formula includes the item price, shipping, and any sales tax collected:

Final Value Fee = (Item Price + Shipping + Sales Tax) × FVF Percentage + $0.30

Example: You sell a jacket for $80 + $10 shipping in Clothing (13.25% FVF).

  • Total sale amount = $80 + $10 = $90
  • FVF = $90 × 13.25% = $11.93
  • Plus per-order fee = $0.30
  • Total eBay fee = $11.93 + $0.30 = $12.23

What's Included in Final Value Fee?

The final value fee covers:

  • Product listing on eBay marketplace
  • Access to eBay's buyer base (159 million active buyers)
  • Checkout and payment processing
  • Seller protection programs
  • Customer dispute resolution

Important: Final value fees are charged regardless of whether you use eBay managed payments or your own payment processing.

Final Value Fee vs. Insertion Fee

eBay has two main types of fees:

  • Insertion Fee: Charged when you list an item (first 250 listings/month are free for most sellers)
  • Final Value Fee: Charged only when the item actually sells

Most sellers only pay final value fees because they stay under the 250 free listings per month threshold.

How to Reduce eBay Final Value Fees

While final value fees are non-negotiable, you can optimize your selling strategy:

  • Choose lower-fee categories: Electronics (12.5%) vs. Clothing (13.25%) saves you money
  • Offer free shipping: Build shipping into your price to avoid fees on shipping charges
  • Use eBay Store subscription: Store subscribers get lower final value fees (as low as 9%)
  • Promote strategically: Promoted listings add 2-8% but can boost sales volume

Common Questions About Final Value Fees

Q: Do I pay final value fee on unsold items?
A: No, final value fees are only charged when an item sells. You may pay an insertion fee for listing, but not FVF.

Q: Is final value fee charged on the total amount or just the item price?
A: On the total amount including item price, shipping, and any sales tax collected from the buyer.

Q: Are final value fees refundable if the buyer returns the item?
A: Yes, eBay refunds the final value fee when a buyer returns an item and receives a full refund.

Q: Do final value fees change every year?
A: Yes, eBay adjusts fees periodically. Always check the current fee schedule before pricing your items.

Final Thoughts

eBay final value fees are the cost of accessing eBay's massive buyer base. The key is understanding your fees so you can price profitably. For most casual sellers, the 13.25% average FVF plus $0.30 per order is reasonable. High-volume sellers should consider an eBay Store subscription to reduce fees.
