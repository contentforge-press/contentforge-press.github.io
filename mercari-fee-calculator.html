The one detail most Mercari sellers get wrong

Mercari's 10% is charged on the combined item price plus any shipping the buyer pays — not on the item alone. On a $40 item with $6 buyer-paid shipping, the fee is 10% of $46 = $4.60, not $4.00. If you offer free shipping instead, the fee applies only to the item price (because the buyer isn't paying separate shipping), but the actual postage cost then comes out of your earnings. Model both scenarios above before you set a price.

Mercari removed the old 2.9% + $0.50 payment processing fee for listings created on or after January 6, 2025, so current sellers pay just the single 10%. Many older guides still list the removed charge; this calculator does not include it. The buyer's 3.6% protection fee is added at checkout and never reduces your payout.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Mercari take in 2026?

A flat 10% selling fee on the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping. There is no separate payment processing fee for listings created or updated after January 6, 2025, and no listing fee.

Does Mercari take 10% of shipping too?

Yes when the buyer pays shipping: the 10% is calculated on the total the buyer pays, including that shipping. If you offer free shipping, the buyer pays no separate shipping so the fee applies only to your item price, but you cover the postage cost yourself.

Is the 3.6% Buyer Protection fee deducted from me?

No. It is charged to the buyer at checkout on top of the item and shipping. It does not reduce your payout, though it can slightly affect how buyers perceive your total price.

How much are Mercari fees on a $50 item?

With no separate shipping, $5.00 (10%), leaving a $45.00 payout. If the buyer also pays, say, $8 shipping, the fee is $5.80 (10% of $58) and your payout is $52.20 before any costs you cover.

Is Mercari cheaper than Poshmark or eBay?

On fees, usually. Mercari's 10% is well below Poshmark's 20% on items $15+ and below eBay's roughly 13.6% plus a per-order charge. But Poshmark and eBay have larger buyer bases in fashion and general goods, which can mean faster sales or higher prices. Compare exact payouts with our Mercari vs Poshmark calculator.
