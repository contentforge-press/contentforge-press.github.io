Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit as a percentage of cost; margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price. A 50% markup gives a 33.3% margin, not 50%. The calculator shows both so you never confuse them.

When should I use target-margin pricing?

Use it when your business plan or a buyer sets a required profit margin. The calculator works backwards to give you the exact price that hits that margin.

How does competitive pricing work?

You anchor on a competitor's price and set yours relative to it. The calculator lets you price above or below the benchmark, then shows the resulting margin so you can see if the position is sustainable.

Is psychological pricing worth it?

Ending prices in .99 or .95 consistently improves conversion for consumer goods in many categories. The calculator rounds down from your target price so you keep a psychological edge while preserving margin.
