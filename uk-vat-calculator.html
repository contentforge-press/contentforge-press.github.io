Frequently asked questions

How do I remove 20% VAT from a total?

Divide the gross price by 1.20 to get the net, then subtract the net from the gross to isolate the VAT. At 20% the VAT is exactly one sixth of the gross (£120 gross = £100 net + £20 VAT).

Why can't I just subtract 20% from the gross?

Because VAT is 20% of the net price, not 20% of the bigger gross price. £600 − 20% = £480 is wrong; the correct net is £600 ÷ 1.20 = £500. This is the single most common VAT error.

When must I register for UK VAT?

You must register when your VAT-taxable turnover exceeds the registration threshold over a rolling 12-month period. The threshold can change, so check the current figure on GOV.UK — registration may also be voluntary even if you are below it.
