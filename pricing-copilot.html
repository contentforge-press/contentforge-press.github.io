How the AI pricing copilot works

Most sellers price forward — pick a number, subtract fees, and hope the profit works out. The copilot prices backward. You give it your landed product cost, the package weight, and the net margin you actually want. It then solves for the selling price on each marketplace that hits that margin after the platform's real 2026 fees, and shows the take-home profit side by side.

  • Amazon FBA & Walmart — referral fee plus size- and weight-based fulfillment fee, including the Oct–Jan peak rates.
  • eBay — category-dependent final value fee and per-order fee.
  • Etsy — listing, 6.5% transaction, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing, with optional Offsite Ads.
  • Shopify — no marketplace commission, but the plan and card-processing costs are included so you can compare honestly.

Because every platform charges differently, the same product rarely has the same optimal price in five places. The recommendation at the end ranks where your margin is highest and where the marketplace brings buyers — not just where the fee looks lowest.

Pricing questions, answered

Why is the price different on every platform?

Each marketplace takes a different mix of referral, payment, and fulfillment fees. Amazon and Walmart add a weight-based FBA/WFS fee; eBay charges a percentage final-value fee; Etsy stacks several small cuts. To keep the same net margin, your selling price has to move with those fees.

Does the highest-margin platform mean the best place to sell?

Not always. Shopify and Depop can show the lowest fee, but you must bring the audience yourself. Amazon, Etsy, and Poshmark charge more yet deliver ready buyers and search traffic. The copilot balances margin against who supplies the demand.

What is reverse pricing and why use it?

Instead of guessing a price and checking profit later, you set the profit you need and the tool calculates the exact price that earns it after fees. This prevents underpricing — the most common reason sellers look busy but earn almost nothing.

Are the fees accurate for 2026?

The models use published 2026 US rates, including FBA peak fees and Etsy's Offsite Ads tiers. Marketplaces occasionally adjust fees by category, so confirm the final figure in your own seller dashboard before listing.
