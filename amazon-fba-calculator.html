Frequently asked questions

How much is the Amazon referral fee in 2026?

Most categories charge 15%, but many are lower — electronics and computers 8%, automotive 12%, and some clothing or low-price items 5–10%. Every referral fee has a $0.30 minimum. Select your category above to use the right rate.

What is the FBA fulfillment fee for a typical 1 lb item?

A large standard item at 1–1.25 lb pays about $4.22 in the non-peak season and $4.52 during the Oct 15 – Jan 14 peak period. The exact figure depends on size tier and weight.

How are FBA fees changing in 2026?

Amazon raised FBA fulfillment fees by an average of about $0.08 per unit; referral fees were not increased. Products priced $10–$50 changed the least, while items above $50 saw larger increases due to extra handling.

What costs do most sellers forget?

Inbound shipping to Amazon, prep-center fees ($1–3/unit), monthly storage, aged-inventory surcharges after about 271 days, and a 3–5% buffer for returns. Add these so your margin is realistic.

How is break-even price calculated?

It is the lowest selling price at which revenue exactly covers the referral fee, fulfillment fee, storage and your landed product cost. Everything above it is profit.
