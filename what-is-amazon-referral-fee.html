What Is Amazon Referral Fee? (2026 Complete Guide)

Amazon referral fees are the commission Amazon charges sellers for every item sold on their marketplace. Think of it as a "finders fee" — Amazon connects you with millions of buyers, and in return, they take a percentage of each sale.

Quick Answer: Amazon referral fees range from 8% to 15% depending on the product category, with most categories at 15%. This fee is charged on the total sale price including shipping.

How to Calculate Amazon Referral Fee

The formula is simple:

Referral Fee = Sale Price × Referral Fee Percentage

Example: You sell a laptop for $500 in the Electronics category (8% referral fee).

  • Referral Fee = $500 × 8% = $40
  • This is what Amazon keeps as commission
  • You receive $500 - $40 = $460 (before other fees)

What's Included in Referral Fee?

The referral fee covers:

  • Access to Amazon's marketplace and customer base
  • Product listing and detail page
  • Checkout and payment processing
  • Customer service for the transaction
  • A-to-z Guarantee protection

Important: Referral fees are separate from FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) fees. If you use FBA, you'll also pay storage and fulfillment fees on top of the referral fee.

Referral Fee vs. Other Amazon Fees

Amazon sellers typically pay three types of fees:

  • Referral Fee: 8-15% commission on each sale
  • FBA Fee: ~$4.22 average for fulfillment (if using FBA)
  • Payment Processing: Included in referral fee (no extra charge)

How to Minimize Referral Fees

While you can't avoid referral fees, you can optimize your profitability:

  • Choose high-margin categories: Electronics (8%) vs. Clothing (17%) makes a big difference
  • Price strategically: Higher prices mean higher absolute fees, but better margins
  • Use FBA wisely: For small, light items, FBA fees are reasonable; for large items, consider FBM
  • Negotiate for high volume: If you sell 10,000+ units/month, contact Amazon about fee discounts

Common Questions About Referral Fees

Q: Do I pay referral fee on returned items?
A: No, Amazon refunds the referral fee when a customer returns an item (though you may lose the FBA return processing fee).

Q: Is referral fee charged on the sale price or profit?
A: On the total sale price including shipping charges, not your profit.

Q: Are referral fees different for professional vs. individual sellers?
A: No, the referral fee percentage is the same. The only difference is the $39.99/month subscription for professional sellers.

Q: Do referral fees change every year?
A: Yes, Amazon occasionally adjusts fees. Always check the current fee schedule before pricing your products.
