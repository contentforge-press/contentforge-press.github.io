Frequently asked questions

What is PayPal's fee for receiving a payment in 2026?

For U.S. accounts, Checkout, Venmo and most commercial payments cost 3.49% + a $0.49 fixed fee.

Is there an extra fee for international payments?

Yes, an additional 1.50% when the sender is in a different market.

What is the Goods and Services fee?

2.99% for U.S. transactions.

What are micropayment fees?

About 5% + $0.05 for eligible digital goods, often cheaper than standard on tiny amounts.
