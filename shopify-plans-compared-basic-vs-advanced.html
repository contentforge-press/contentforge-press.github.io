Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify plan is best for a new store?

Most new stores start on Basic at $39/month and upgrade later when higher volume makes the lower processing rates worthwhile.

Do higher plans lower payment fees?

Yes. The per-order card rate and the third-party gateway surcharge both decrease on more expensive plans.

When should I upgrade plans?

When the monthly savings from lower fees on your sales volume exceed the added subscription cost.
