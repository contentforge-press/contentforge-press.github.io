How Vinted fees work (2026)

Vinted is the only major resale marketplace with truly zero seller fees:

  • Commission: 0%
  • Listing fee: $0.00
  • Payment processing: $0.00
  • Buyer protection fee: 5% + $0.70 (paid by buyer, not you)

The buyer pays a "Buyer Protection fee" of about 5% + $0.70 on top of the listed price. This does not come out of your earnings.
