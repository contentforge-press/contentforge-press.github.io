Frequently asked questions

What are total FBA fees on a $30 item?

The 15% referral fee is $4.50 and small-standard FBA fulfillment is about $4.22, for roughly $8.72 total before your product and ad costs.

Does the referral fee change by category?

Yes. 15% is typical, but categories range from about 8% (some electronics) to 45% (Amazon device accessories).

Is storage included?

No. Monthly storage (about $0.87 per cubic foot) and any inbound or prep costs are extra.
