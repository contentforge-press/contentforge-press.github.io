Frequently asked questions

What is economic nexus?

It is the connection established once your sales into a state cross that state's threshold, requiring you to register and collect sales tax.

Are thresholds the same everywhere?

No. Most states use $100,000, but some are lower or higher, and a few also count the number of transactions.

What should online sellers do?

Track sales by state and register once you approach a threshold, using a reliable sales-tax tool to stay compliant.
