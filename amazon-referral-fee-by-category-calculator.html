How the calculator works

The referral fee formula is simple: Referral fee = Total sales price × Category rate, or the minimum per-item fee — whichever is greater. "Total sales price" includes the item price, delivery charges, and any gift wrap. Note: this is only the referral fee — FBA fulfillment fees ($3–$7+ per unit depending on size/weight) are separate. Frequently asked questions

What is the Amazon referral fee in 2026?

Amazon referral fees range from 5% to 45% depending on category. Most common categories (Home, Toys, Sports, Tools) are 15%. Electronics and Computers are 8%. Clothing is 5–17% depending on price tier. The minimum referral fee is $0.30 per item in most categories.

How do I calculate Amazon referral fee?

Referral fee = (item price + shipping + gift wrap) × category percentage, or the minimum fee ($0.30), whichever is greater. For example, a $25 toy: $25 × 15% = $3.75. A $5 electronic: $5 × 8% = $0.40 (greater than $0.30 minimum).

Is the referral fee the same as the FBA fee?

No. The referral fee is charged on every sale regardless of fulfillment method. FBA fulfillment fees are additional and charged only when Amazon stores and ships your product. FBA fees are based on size tier and weight, typically $3–$7+ per standard unit.

Does Amazon waive referral fees for new sellers?

Yes. New-to-Amazon brands can get over $50,000 in referral fee waivers in their first year. This applies to the first $50K+ in GMV depending on the current promotion.
