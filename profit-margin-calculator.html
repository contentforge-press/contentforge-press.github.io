Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin = profit ÷ price; markup = profit ÷ cost. Markup is always the larger figure.

How do I calculate a 30% profit margin?

Divide cost by 0.70. A $21 cost needs a $30 price.

What is a good profit margin for online sellers?

40–60% gross margin is a common target; factor platform fees and ads in separately.
