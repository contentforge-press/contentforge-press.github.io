Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax rate in Alabama?

The state rate is 4%. Local governments add an average of 5.46%, so the typical combined rate is about 9.46%.

Do I have to collect sales tax as an out-of-state seller?

Remote sellers establish economic nexus in Alabama at $250,000 in annual sales (no transaction count). $250,000 in sales, no transaction count. Once you cross the threshold you generally must register and begin collecting on sales delivered into Alabama.

How is tax calculated on a sale?

Multiply the pre-tax price by the combined rate (state + local). For example, a $100 sale at the average 9.46% rate carries $9.46 of tax, for a total of $109.46.
